Dataset overview
================

- `Hawea_Updated_RSWetlands.shp <../../Hawea_Updated_RSWetlands.shp>`_: shapefile to locate wetlands
- `NGMP bore fluctuations 1996 - 2019.csv <../../NGMP bore fluctuations 1996 - 2019.csv>`_: NGMP well data
- `Peizo Survey 20Sept2011.xlsx <../../Peizo Survey 20Sept2011.xlsx>`_: Results of the 2011 piezometer survey (Wilson, 2012)
- `daily_head_obs.csv <../../daily_head_obs.csv>`_: Daily head observations for the 5 high frequency bores
